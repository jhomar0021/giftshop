The Hearts Behind Amour & Co.

How we grew from a small backyard flower bench to a sanctuary for thoughtful lovers everywhere.

The Humble Beginning

Amour & Co. started with nothing but a rolling kitchen block, a spool of silk crimson ribbon, and a vision. Founded by high school sweethearts after struggling to find meaningful personalized keepsakes, we set out to build an intentional alternative to generic gifts.

We made it our mission to bridge local greenhouse florals with luxury artisans. Every package leaves our studio designed to deliver butterflies.

Our Design Sanctuary

Today, our primary brick-and-mortar studio houses an olfactory bar, zero-waste wrapping stations, and an engineering suite for processing preserved florals that remain vibrant for up to three years without water.

We carefully control every parameter—from environmental storage down to individual box presentation tissue layer structures.
